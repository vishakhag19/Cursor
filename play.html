Fun principles

Play has rules, too.

  1. Make the invisible visible. Good play exposes assumptions without turning the room defensive.
  2. Use constraints generously. A small box can make teams bolder because the risk feels bounded.
  3. Leave with a sharper question. The best playful exercise creates momentum for the real product decision waiting nearby.
