Case study 01

Business banking onboarding

A regional bank needed a digital application that reduced branch handoffs while still meeting strict KYC and compliance needs.

Problem

Applicants abandoned the process when legal language, document requests, and unclear progress made them question whether they were eligible.

Approach

I mapped the end-to-end service, interviewed branch associates, ran moderated prototype tests, and created a modular form system with contextual help.

Outcome

Completion time dropped by 38%, support calls decreased, and the team gained a reusable pattern library for future account products.

Case study 02

Caregiver coordination app

Families needed a calmer way to coordinate appointments, medications, and updates without turning every change into a group chat emergency.

Problem

Important tasks were scattered across text threads, calendars, and paper notes, creating duplicate work and missed follow-up.

Approach

I led diary studies, co-designed task states with caregivers, and tested notification tone to find a balance between urgency and emotional load.

Outcome

The pilot improved weekly task clarity scores and established a warm, trustworthy interaction model for the broader product.

Case study 03

Team planning workspace

A distributed product organization wanted planning rituals that worked across time zones without adding another status meeting.

Problem

Decisions lived in multiple tools, priorities shifted silently, and teams lacked a single source of truth for tradeoffs.

Approach

I facilitated opportunity mapping, prototyped async planning loops, and paired with engineering to define telemetry around decision confidence.

Outcome

The beta reduced planning follow-up meetings and became the foundation for a new collaboration product line.

Process

A flexible design loop for high-ambiguity product work.

01

Frame the tension

Align the team around the user problem, business constraint, and riskiest assumption before jumping into solutions.

02

Make behavior visible

Use journey maps, storyboards, and prototypes to turn fragmented insights into shared product decisions.

03

Test the moment

Validate the moments that carry trust, comprehension, and momentum with representative users.

04

Ship the system

Document reusable patterns, accessibility expectations, and success metrics so the work keeps improving after launch.
